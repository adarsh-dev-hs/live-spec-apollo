The component register for the Apollo platform. The delivery plan is organised by **when work is sold
and scheduled** (milestones M0–M15). This register is the **engineering translation**: organised by
**what gets built and deployed**. Every component below maps back to one or more plan items
(`M<n>-I<k>`), and every one of the plan's 208 items appears against exactly one component.

---

## 1. How the two organisations differ

| Plan (`requirement_doc`) | Docs (`docs/`) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| M0 Platform Foundations | split across `02-01`…`02-04` | Six unrelated capabilities sold as one milestone; each is its own deployable. |
| M1 Foundations & Migration | `02-05`, `02-06`, `02-13`, `02-15`, `03-13`, `05-05` | Design system, console shell, adapter layer, environments, migration and the D11 documentation obligations have nothing in common but a delivery slot. |
| M3 Demand Intake & Allocation | `02-08`, `02-09`, `02-11`, `03-02`, `03-03` | The shared inbound stack, the message sender and the event spine are consumed by M5/M7/M8/M9 — they are platform, not demand. |
| M4 Candidate Record | `02-12`, `03-04`, `03-05` | The Ops-side record and the React Native app are two deployables on two release cadences. |
| M5 Engagement | `02-09`, `03-06` | Templates/sender/retry/log are platform messaging; cadence/decay/response capture are the module. |
| M7 Selection, Offer & Client Response | `02-14`, `03-08` | A13 is a shared mechanism used by M7, M8 and M9. |
| M9 Billing, Revenue & Reporting | `03-11`, `03-12` | The plan itself says these two workstreams share nothing but the event data. |
| M15 Timesheet Ops & Collections | `03-19`, `03-20` | Two unrelated extensions of two different modules. |

**Nothing is added and nothing is dropped.** Where a component exists that the plan does not name, it
is marked **[engineering addition]** with a justification.
