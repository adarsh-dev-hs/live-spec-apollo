## 1. What the business does

Apollo Knowledge Services places healthcare professionals — **nurses, doctors and allied health** — into
**hospitals and home healthcare providers**. It runs two lines of business at once, and they behave
completely differently:

| | Permanent placement | Contractual (shift) staffing |
|---|---|---|
| What is sold | A person, hired by the client | Hours worked, invoiced by the shift |
| Supply base | **Base A** — the permanent pool | **Base B** — the shift pool |
| Availability means | A notice period | Time windows within a travel radius |
| How Apollo is paid | 5–6% of CTC, invoiced **at day 90** once the client confirms the person is still there | Shift-level invoice from a **client-approved timesheet** |
| Billing trigger | `RetentionRecord` (A7) produced by a client clicking a link | `Timesheet` in state `approved` or `deemed_approved` |
| Attendance | Not tracked — the person is the client's employee | App check-in, the **only** route (D3/D22) |
| Payroll | Not Apollo's | Apollo's, run by hand until Phase 7 (D24) |

A locum doctor is a *contractual* engagement even though the candidate category is doctor. **The billing
trigger follows the service line, not the candidate category** (M9-I1, client letter item 9).

## 3. The two value flows, end to end

### Permanent (18 stages)

```
client onboarded → contract signed (terms + windows) → requisition arrives on one of 5 channels
  → normalised, completeness-scored, chased on the originating channel
  → owner set, recruiters allocated on the morning call
  → ranked shortlist produced (weights by category × base)
  → shortlist sent to client by tokenised link → client accepts / rejects / requests interview
  → interview proposed, held, structured feedback captured
  → accept / hold / reject with a mandatory structured reason  ──▶ writes back into ranking (A8)
  → offer generated from template, delivered by WhatsApp, e-signed, mapped to contract version
  → joining confirmed (doctors: only after privileging granted) → placement opens, 90-day clock starts
  → trial check-ins at day 0, 7, 30, 60, 90 (candidate side and client side)
  → day 90: client clicks link, or the deemed-confirmation window expires
  → RetentionRecord written, immutable  ──▶  permanent invoice raised, receivable opened
```

### Contractual (10 stages)

```
client onboarded → contract with shift rates and deemed-approval window
  → shift requisition + shift template → one ShiftDemandLine per shift
  → candidates ranked against the shift line (Base B weights, travel radius, no clash)
  → candidate accepts the shift in the app
  → candidate checks in / out in the app, location stamped but not enforced
  → attendance exceptions resolved → timesheet compiled and priced against the contract version
  → timesheet sent to client by tokenised link → approved, or deemed approved after the window
  → shift invoice raised → receivable opened
```

Both chains stop dead without the tokenised link (`02-14`). The contractual chain stops dead without
the mobile app (`03-05`) — there is no alternative attendance route.

## 5. Category and care setting are dimensions, not releases

The client letter of 19 August is explicit: the core must be **category-aware and care-setting-aware from
M0**, held as configurable reference data rather than in code. Three categories × two supply bases gives
**six supply segments**, each with its own acquisition motion, decay schedule, contact cadence and
ranking weight set.

| Category | A9 attribute set |
|---|---|
| Nurse | specialty, acuity |
| Doctor | specialty, case-mix, grade |
| Allied health | discipline, equipment, technique competency |

| Care setting | Consequences |
|---|---|
| Hospital | Has departments, a preceptor, a fixed site; geo-fence reference is the site |
| Home healthcare | **No department, no preceptor, no accommodation**; the workplace is a patient residence that changes between shifts, so the visit address sits on the shift line and is the geo-fence reference (`OI-HS-17`, `OI-HS-18`) |

A core built to one category makes doctors a migration rather than a module. This is why the reference
data lives in `02-03` and the attribute sets in `03-04`, and why nothing hardcodes a category anywhere.

## 7. What the platform is not

- Not the accounting system of record. The chain ends at *invoice raised, receivable open* until Phase 7.
- Not a payroll calculator. A third-party engine calculates; the platform sends inputs and stores outputs.
- Not a filing agent. PF/ESIC reports and challans are produced; filing is outside the contract.
- Not an LMS. Certification records are held and can be received; the LMS is Apollo's own build.
- Not a client-facing analytics product. Clients see links until Phase 6 and the portal after it.
