*M12 · D23 · Months 5–6*

`Release 2` · `6 items` · `26 conditions` · `38.0pd` · `0 high risk` · `Ops/Admin, Candidate App`

The day-to-day changes that happen once people are working: salary revisions for one person or in bulk, salary advances, and putting a salary on hold or releasing it. Each of these needs two people — one to request it and one to approve it — so nobody can change their own pay. It also covers uploading attendance and pairing it against what was verified, and this is where location checking on shift check-in finally gets switched on and enforced.

### Built once, used elsewhere

The request-and-approve pattern built here is the same one M15 uses for timesheet approvals. Build it once.

### Records this milestone owns

- WorkforceRequest — one request to change something, with who asked, who approved, and the before and after values.
- SalaryRevision — a change to what someone is paid, for one person or a batch.
- SalaryAdvance, SalaryHold — money paid early, or pay stopped and later released.
- AttendanceUpload — attendance brought in from outside and matched against what the app recorded.
- GeoFence — a boundary around a site, used to accept or reject a shift check-in.

### How records move

- WorkforceRequest: drafted → submitted → approved or rejected with a reason → applied
- SalaryHold: requested → approved → held → released
- Check-in with enforcement on: inside the boundary → accepted. Outside → rejected, or flagged for someone to review.

### Not in this milestone

- Actually paying the money. That is M13.
- Statutory filings.

### Needs to exist first

- M8 for placements and attendance. M11 for the associate profile. M1 for roles, since approvals depend on who someone is.

### What breaks without it

- M13 payroll reads approved salary changes, advances and holds.

#### M12-I1 — Requests

`D23` · `Screen` · `L · 3.0pd` · `AI High` · `Medium` · `SHARED` · `Low`

**Who uses it: Ops, Fulfilment lead.** One place for every change that needs a second person to approve it. The maker-checker engine Part A names.

##### Screens to build

1. **Requests**
  - Lists changes waiting to be approved with what they would change
  - Approves or rejects a request with a reason
  - Stops the person who raised it approving it

##### Conditions it must satisfy

- A request cannot be approved by the person who raised it
- Every approval and rejection records who, when and why (M0-I4)
- The before and after values are shown so the approver sees what changes
- This same engine is reused for internal timesheet approval in M15

#### M12-I5 — Geo-fence settings

`D23` · `Config` · `L · 3.0pd` · `AI High` · `Medium` · `SPEC` · `Low`

**Who uses it: Platform administrator.** Switching on the location check that M8 records but does not act on. Part A names this as a later-phase item.

##### Screens to build

1. **Geo-fence settings**
  - Sets a boundary around each site for facility work, and around the visit address for home visits
  - Sets whether a check-in outside the boundary is allowed, warned or blocked
  - Sets the tolerance, since a phone indoors is less accurate than one outside

##### Conditions it must satisfy

- With enforcement off, behaviour is exactly as in M8 — location recorded, nothing blocked
- With enforcement on, a check-in outside the boundary is rejected or flagged per the mode
- The location field already exists from M8, so switching this on needs no data change
- A breach report shows attempts outside the boundary
- The same check runs for both cases; only the reference point differs - the site record for facility work, the visit address on the shift line for home visits
- Tolerance is set wide enough that a phone indoors does not produce false rejections

**Who uses it: System.** The shared approval mechanism behind every workforce change.

##### Screens to build
