*M15 · D22 (full), D25 (full) · Months 6–6*

`Release 2` · `7 items` · `27 conditions` · `44.0pd` · `0 high risk` · `Ops/Admin, Client link`

Two things left out of the thin slices. First, the full timesheet: leave types, overtime rules, and an internal approval route inside Apollo before it goes to the client. Second, the money coming back in: recording payments received, matching them against invoices including part-payments and TDS, an overdue report, automatic reminders and chasing, and credit and debit notes. Together these turn the platform from one that raises invoices into one that tracks whether Apollo actually got paid.

### Built once, used elsewhere

Internal timesheet approval uses the same request-and-approve pattern built in M12. Chasing reminders use the messaging layer from M5.

### Records this milestone owns

- LeaveType, LeaveRecord — categories of absence and who took what.
- OvertimeRule — when extra hours are paid at a different rate.
- TimesheetApprovalRoute — who inside Apollo must approve before it reaches the client.
- Payment, PaymentAllocation — money received, and which invoices it was matched to.
- CreditNote, DebitNote — corrections to an invoice already raised.
- DunningSchedule — when and how overdue invoices get chased.

### How records move

- Timesheet gains: built → internally approved → sent to client → client approved → invoiced
- Invoice: raised → partly paid → fully paid. Or credited.
- Payment: received → matched to invoices → any remainder left unmatched

### Not in this milestone

- Being the accounting system of record. The finance system stays the book of record; this tracks against it.

### Needs to exist first

- M8 for timesheets. M9 for invoices and receivables. M2 for contract payment terms.

### What breaks without it

- M13 payroll reads the fuller timesheet including leave and overtime.

#### M15-I1 — Leave and overtime rules

`D22` · `Config` · `L · 3.0pd` · `AI High` · `Medium` · `SPEC` · `Low`

**Who uses it: Finance, Platform administrator.** The categories and rules the thin-slice timesheet in M8 deliberately leaves out.

##### Screens to build

1. **Leave and overtime rules**
  - Lists the leave types and the overtime rules
  - Sets when extra hours are paid at a different rate

##### Conditions it must satisfy

- Leave types are configurable, not hardcoded
- Overtime rules can differ by client and by state
- A rule change does not alter timesheets already approved

#### M15-I2 — Internal timesheet approval

`D22` · `Screen` · `L · 3.0pd` · `AI High` · `Medium` · `XMOD` · `Low`

**Who uses it: Fulfilment lead, Finance.** An Apollo-side approval before the timesheet goes to the client. M8 sends straight to the client; this adds a step in front.

##### Screens to build

1. **Internal timesheet approval**
  - Lists timesheets waiting for Apollo approval before they go to the client
  - Approves one, or returns it with a reason

##### Conditions it must satisfy

- A timesheet is approved internally before the client link is issued (M8-I8 extension)
- Routing uses the same approval engine built in M12
- Returning a timesheet stops it reaching the client until corrected
- The client-side waiting period only starts once the client link is actually sent

#### M15-I3 — Payments received

`D25` · `Screen` · `XL · 5.0pd` · `AI High` · `Medium` · `XMOD` · `Low`

**Who uses it: Finance.** Recording money that arrives and matching it against invoices, including part-payments and tax deducted at source.

##### Screens to build

1. **Payments received**
  - Records money received against invoices
  - Matches a payment across several invoices, and part payments
  - Records tax deducted at source
  - Lists payments that have not been matched

##### Conditions it must satisfy

- A payment can be matched across several invoices, and an invoice can take several payments
- TDS is recorded separately so the gross and net are both visible
- An unmatched payment sits in a queue rather than being forced onto the nearest invoice
- Matching is audited because it changes what appears as outstanding

`D25` · `Screen` · `L · 3.0pd` · `AI High` · `Low` · `XMOD` · `Low`

#### M15-I5 — Credit and debit notes

`D25` · `Screen` · `L · 3.0pd` · `AI High` · `Medium` · `SPEC` · `Low`

**Who uses it: Finance.** Correcting an invoice already raised. Part A says these return to scope if the guarantee period runs longer than the 90-day trial.

##### Screens to build

1. **Credit and debit notes**
