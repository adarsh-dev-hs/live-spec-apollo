*M10 · D12 · Months 5–6*

`Release 2` · `8 items` · `30 conditions` · `44.0pd` · `1 high risk` · `Client Portal`

A proper logged-in website for clients. Until now clients have only had one-off links with no password. This gives them accounts: they can raise vacancies themselves, see how their vacancies are progressing, look at shortlists, respond to candidates, approve timesheets and download invoices. Once this exists, the client portal also becomes a sixth way requisitions arrive, which M3 already allows for in its channel list.

### Built once, used elsewhere

This adds a whole new group of users. The role and permission model from M1 has to stretch to cover people outside Apollo, and every screen needs to be checked so a client only ever sees their own data.

### Records this milestone owns

- ClientUser — a login for someone at the client. Separate from Apollo staff logins.
- ClientUserRole — what that person can do and which sites they can see.
- PortalSession — logins and activity, kept for audit.

### How records move

- ClientUser: invited → registered → active → suspended

### Not in this milestone

- Clients editing their own rate card or contract terms. Those stay with Apollo.
- Clients seeing candidate personal data beyond what the shortlist already shows.

### Needs to exist first

- M1 for the permission model, extended to outside users. M2 for the client and contact records. M3, M6, M7, M8 and M9 for everything the portal displays.

### What breaks without it

- M3 gains a sixth intake channel. The one-off links from M7 stay in place for clients who never sign in.

### Items — 8 to build · design 10.5pd, build 22.0pd, QA 11.5pd

#### M10-I1 — Client sign in

`D12` · `Screen` · `L · 3.0pd` · `AI High` · `Medium` · `COMP` · `Low`

**Who uses it: Client user.** The first client login the platform has ever had. Clients receive an invite, set a password and sign in.

##### Screens to build

1. **Client sign in**
  - Takes an email address and a password
  - Signs the client user in and opens the portal
2. **Set up account**
  - Opened from an invitation
  - Takes a password and activates the account

##### Conditions it must satisfy

- A client user completes set-up from an invite and can sign in afterwards
- An expired invite can be reissued without creating a second account
- A client user cannot reach any Apollo internal screen
- Sessions expire and require a fresh login
- Client identity is kept entirely separate from Apollo staff identity

#### M10-I2 — Portal users

`D12` · `Screen` · `M · 2.0pd` · `AI High` · `Medium` · `COMP` · `Low`

**Who uses it: Client admin, Apollo BD.** Someone at the client adds and removes their own colleagues, within limits Apollo sets.

##### Screens to build

1. **Portal users**
  - Lists the users at that client with their access
  - Invites a colleague
  - Removes a user

##### Conditions it must satisfy

- A client admin can only see and manage users belonging to their own client
- Site-level access limits what a user sees to their own hospitals
- Apollo can suspend a client user without going through the client
- Removing a user does not remove the records they created

#### M10-I3 — Portal: raise requisition

`D12` · `Screen` · `L · 3.0pd` · `AI High` · `Medium` · `XMOD` · `Low`

**Who uses it: Client user.** The sixth intake channel. M3 already lists it and leaves it switched off; this switches it on.

##### Screens to build

1. **Raise a requisition**
  - Takes the same fields as the internal requisition form
  - Limits sites and departments to that client
  - Submits it, which creates a requisition marked as arriving by portal

##### Conditions it must satisfy

- A requisition raised here produces the same record as one arriving by email (M3 AC1)
- The source channel is recorded as portal
- Enabling this channel is configuration in M3, not a schema change (M3 AS-05)
- The client only sees their own sites and departments
- Completeness is checked at submit so the chase loop is rarely needed

`D12` · `Screen` · `M · 2.0pd` · `AI High` · `Low` · `XMOD` · `Low`

**Who uses it: Client user.** Clients see how their own vacancies are going without phoning a recruiter.

#### M10-I8 — Portal data scoping

`D12` · `Service` · `XL · 5.0pd` · `AI Medium` · `High` · `COMP` · `Low`

**Who uses it: System.** Every portal query must be limited to the signed-in user's client and sites. One mistake here leaks another hospital's data.

##### Screens to build

1. **No screen**
  - Limits every portal request to the signed-in user's own client and sites
  - Refuses any request that has not been limited

##### Conditions it must satisfy

- No portal query can return data belonging to another client, under any parameter
- A user limited to two sites cannot see a third
- A new portal endpoint added later fails closed if scoping is not applied
- Scoping is tested automatically, not by inspection
