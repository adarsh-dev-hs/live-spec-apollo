`Release 1` · `16 items` · `89 conditions` · `107.5pd` · `12 high risk` · `Ops/Admin, Candidate App, WhatsApp, Voice`

Apollo needs to know which nurses are actually available right now. This module decides who to contact, how often, and on which channel, then sends the message over WhatsApp, SMS, email, app push or an automated phone call. When the person answers, it records the answer in one standard format and resets a freshness score on their record. If nobody hears from them, that score decays over time, and M6 ranks them lower as a result. The shift pool gets contacted every three to four days; the permanent pool less often.

### Built once, used elsewhere

Four pieces here only work as a set: the engine that picks who to contact, the sending layer, the reply capture, and the freshness score. Build any three of them and none of them does anything useful. Estimate and schedule them together, not separately.

### Records this milestone owns

- NotificationTemplate — the message text per channel. WhatsApp templates also need approval from the provider before they can be sent, which takes time.
- NotificationLog — one row per message sent, per person, with whether it arrived.
- ContactPreference, OptOut — which channels a candidate is willing to be contacted on.
- Segment, CadenceRule — a group of candidates and how often to contact them.
- AvailabilityResponse — the reply, in one format whether it came from WhatsApp, the app or a phone call.
- VoiceCall, VoiceTranscript — the automated call log and what was said.
- AvailabilityConfidence — a freshness score. It lives on the candidate record in M4, is written here, and is read by M6.

### How records move

- Notification: queued → sent → delivered. Or failed → retried → given up on.
- AvailabilityConfidence: confirmed (reset to full) → decaying on a schedule → stale → picked up again by the contact engine
- AvailabilityResponse: received → understood clearly, or unclear and sent to a person to check → confirmed
- VoiceCall: queued → dialling → connected → finished with an answer. Or no answer → tried again later.

### Not in this milestone

- Handling replies to SMS and email. We send on those channels but only WhatsApp and the app can receive a structured reply — see open item OI-A-17.
- Step-by-step guided conversations over WhatsApp. WhatsApp sends messages and fixed-button templates; anything guided happens in the app.
- Reporting on campaign performance beyond the send log. That reporting sits in M9.
- The phone-call service itself. We build the integration and the answer extraction; Apollo buys the service — and has not yet said from whom.

### Needs to exist first

- M4 for the candidate records and the fields availability is written onto. M3 for the message pipeline the reply capture reuses.

### What breaks without it

- M6 ranks on the freshness score. M8 sends trial check-ins through this layer. M7 sends offers and client links through it. If reply capture fails, nothing ever resets and the whole pool goes stale.
