*M7 · D6, D20, A13 · Months 4–5*

`Release 1` · `18 items` · `104 conditions` · `126.5pd` · `10 high risk` · `Ops/Admin, Client link`

This covers interviews, the decision, and the offer. Propose interview slots, handle changes and cancellations, record feedback, then accept, hold or reject with a reason picked from a list. If accepted, generate the offer from a template, send it over WhatsApp, track it, chase it, and get it signed. It also builds the way clients interact with the platform: a one-off web link sent to them over the channel they already use. There is no client login anywhere in this milestone. The same link mechanism is used four ways — responding to a shortlist, three trial check-ins, and approving a timesheet — but only the 90-day one causes an invoice to be raised.

### Built once, used elsewhere

Build the link mechanism once, then configure four types on top of it. Three other modules use those links, so build it before them. Be careful that the day 30 and day 60 links never gain the power to trigger billing — only day 90 does.

### Records this milestone owns

- Interview — slot, format, panel and state, plus the history of any changes.
- InterviewFeedback — structured feedback from each interviewer.
- SelectionDecision — accept, hold or reject, with a reason from the configured list.
- RejectionReason — the configured list. Shared with the ranking feedback in M6.
- Offer, OfferVersion — the document, picked by state, role and skill, with an expiry date and reminders.
- ClientLink — a one-off web address: its type, who it went to, when it was sent, opened and answered.
- LinkRecipient — the person at the client who received it, recorded so the answer can be attributed. How these are held is not yet decided — see open item OI-A-12.

### How records move

- Interview: proposed → confirmed → held → feedback recorded. Or rescheduled, or cancelled with a reason.
- SelectionDecision: pending → accepted, held, or rejected with a reason
- Offer: draft → generated → sent → opened → signed. Or expired, or declined. Then linked to the contract.
- ClientLink: issued → sent → opened → answered. Or expired → treated as agreed (only for the day-90 and timesheet types, and only after the waiting period in the contract).

### Not in this milestone

- Any client login or client account. Links need no password. The portal is milestone M10.
- AI video interviews. Later phase.
- Connecting to interviewers' external calendars. Slots are proposed and confirmed inside the platform.
- Running background checks at offer stage. Verification happens earlier, in M4.
- Letting the day 30 or day 60 link trigger an invoice. Those capture information only.

### Needs to exist first

- M6 for the shortlist. M2 for the contract to map the offer to and the waiting periods. M5 to send offers and links.

### What breaks without it

- M8 needs the accepted offer to create a joining event — without offers the whole permanent flow stops after selection. M9 needs the day-90 link answer as its billing trigger. M6 gets rejection reasons back. If the link mechanism is late, the client side of M7 and both invoice paths in M9 stop, because there is no other way to reach a client.
