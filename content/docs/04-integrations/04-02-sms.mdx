**Package** `@apollo/integration-sms` · **Schema** `messaging` · **Plan** D10 · **Effort** ~2.0pd (within `M5-I8`) · **Risk** Medium `EXT` · **Build in** Phase 3

## 1. Purpose

The fallback channel and the primary OTP route. SMS reaches a candidate who has not installed the app
and has not replied on WhatsApp. In India every commercial SMS requires **DLT registration** of the
sender header and each template with the telecom regulator — a lead time, not a build.

## 2. Scope

**In scope:** sending through an aggregator behind the `02-09` channel interface; DLT template id
carried on each send; delivery receipts; suppression of invalid numbers.

**Explicitly out of scope:** template authoring (`02-09`); OTP generation and rate limiting (`02-01`);
**inbound SMS**, which is out of scope entirely in Release 1 (`OI-A-17`).

**Explicitly not built:** two-way SMS conversations; SMS as an availability response channel; long-code
inbound handling.

## 3. Plan traceability

No item of its own. SMS is one of the four channels inside `M5-I8` (message sender) and `M5-I9` (retry
and receipts), and the delivery route for candidate OTP in `M4-I22`.

## 4. Dependencies

**Must exist first:** `02-09`, `02-01` (OTP).

**Consumed by:** `02-01` OTP, `02-09` channel fallback, `03-06` cadence where a segment prefers SMS.

**External:** `X02` — **DLT registration with the Indian telecom regulator.** Start week one; no amount
of delivery capacity shortens it.

## 5. Data model

No tables. `messaging.template` carries the DLT template id in `provider_template_name`;
`messaging.message.cost_units` counts segments.

## 6. State machines

As `02-09`'s `Message`. One SMS-specific rule: **a DLT-rejected template is a permanent failure**, not
a transient one — it falls to the next channel in the trigger's order rather than retrying.

## 7. API surface

In-process `sms.send()` only, called by `02-09`. No inbound endpoint in Release 1.

## 8. Events and jobs

`sms.send` on the `provider` queue class. Receipts reconcile through `02-09`'s
`messaging.retry-and-receipts` job.

## 9. Frontend

**N/A** — templates and channel order are `02-09`.

## 10. Acceptance criteria

| # | Criterion | Traces to |
|---|---|---|
| AC1 | A send carries its DLT template id and sender header | `X02` |
| AC2 | A DLT rejection is treated as permanent and falls back to the next channel | `M5-I2`, `M5-I9` |
| AC3 | OTP delivery works over SMS **with WhatsApp fallback** | `M4-I22` |
| AC4 | An invalid number is suppressed and flagged on the candidate record rather than retried indefinitely | `M5-I9` |
| AC5 | Segment counts are recorded for cost attribution | `OI-HS-05` |
| AC6 | An inbound SMS reply is recorded and routed to manual entry rather than parsed | `OI-A-17` |

## 11. Open questions

| Ref | Question | Working assumption | Blocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| `X02` | DLT registration — sender header and per-template registration | **Week one.** Regulator lead time. | OTP and cadence fallback in Phase 3 |
