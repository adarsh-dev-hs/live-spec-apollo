Every deliverable in scope, as listed in SOW §1.1. A deliverable carried partly under one reference
and partly under another appears twice, once for each, marked **(thin)** and **(full)**.

**D15, D17, D19, D26 and D27 are descoped from this SOW and do not appear.**

- **Provenance** — where the deliverable came from: the client's original R1 list, moved or deferred
  from R2, a thin slice taken out of R2, or defined by Hiresense because the client's list assumes it
  without naming it.
- **Surface** — where a user meets it: the Ops/Admin console, the candidate app, a tokenised client
  link, the client portal, WhatsApp, voice, or all of them.
- **Parts** — which of the three release parts work on it falls in. More than one part means the
  deliverable is split.

## 8. Scope notes

The SOW's own note against each deliverable. These are the boundary: where a note says a thing is
carried, it is in scope; where it says a thing is not, it is a Change.

### Platform prerequisites

- **F01 · Login and session handling** — How an Apollo user gets into the platform and stays in.
  D11 asks for role-based access but never says how anyone signs in.
- **F02 · User management** — Creating and managing the Apollo staff who use the platform. Demand
  management requires a requisition owner from *the Apollo internal user list*; nothing else creates
  that list.
- **F03 · Roles and permissions** — What each role can see and do. D11 says *role-based access
  control* as a phrase; the actual permission model, and whether a recruiter sees only their own
  site, is not specified anywhere.
- **F04 · Audit trail** — One place that records who did what and when. Several deliverables need
  this — requisition status changes, selection decisions, client link responses — and building it
  six times would be six different answers.
- **F05 · Settings and lists administration** — One admin area for every configurable list and
  waiting period. There are at least nine of them; without a shared home they become nine separate
  screens.
- **F06 · Document and file storage** — Where every uploaded file lives: CVs, certificates, ID
  scans, WhatsApp voice notes, signed offers, invoices.
- **F07 · Message sending infrastructure** — The plumbing underneath every message the platform
  sends. D10 covers templates and preferences; the queue, retries and delivery receipts underneath
  are assumed.
- **F08 · Lists, search, filters and export** — The shared table component every Ops screen uses.
  Around twenty screens in this plan are lists; building filtering and paging separately in each is
  the same work twenty times.
- **F10 · Environments, pipeline and monitoring** — Four environments, a way to move code between
  them, and enough monitoring to know when something has broken.
- **F12 · Design system and component library** — The shared visual language and the components
  built from it. Five different surfaces in this plan; if each is designed separately the work is
  done five times and looks like it.
- **F13 · Data protection: consent, residency, retention** — Handling personal and health-related
  data about candidates in a way that meets Indian data protection law.
- **F14 · Event recording for reporting** — Every module writes an event as things happen, and the
  reports read those events later. **This is the one prerequisite that cannot be added afterwards.**

### Clients, contracts and demand

- **D1 · Client and contract management** — Client entity, locations, departments, rate cards by
  role, skill and location. Care setting held on the client and on the site, distinguishing hospital
  from home healthcare. Sender resolution in A1 matches against this base.
- **D13 · Contract creation and versioning** — Contract record with versioning, renewal alerts,
  history; the deemed-confirmation and deemed-approval windows the billing triggers read.
- **D2 · Demand management** — Requisition object and lifecycle: creation, versioning, hold, revise,
  close; shift templates; fulfilment board and aging. Care setting held on the requisition, with a
  per-visit address on the shift line where the workplace changes between shifts.
- **A1 · Multi-channel demand ingestion** — Five live channels plus verbal capture, all normalising
  to one requisition schema. Owns the inbound stack consumed by A14.
- **D14 (thin) · Prioritisation and allocation** — Owner field and capture of the allocation made on
  the daily morning call. **No engine, no approval workflow.**
- **D14 (full) · Prioritisation and allocation — remainder** — The prioritisation engine, suggested
  recruiter allocation with load balancing, and the requisition approval workflow.

### Candidates, verification and the market layer

- **D3 · Candidate mobile app** — Three-way shared build. Self-registration, resume parsing, dedupe,
  job and shift discovery, application tracking, shift check-in. Carries a configurable set of basic
  screening questions asked at application, with the answers collected against the candidate.
  **Hard dependency of the contractual invoice chain.**
- **D4 · Candidate verification (KYC)** — Scan-based capture and identity verification: Aadhar, PAN
  and certificates read from a scan behind the A12 adapter layer. Which attributes are verified is
  configurable by candidate category, by destination taken from the demand, and by the regulatory
  requirements of the placement and supply locations.
- **D16 · Verification — extended** — Credential object with issuer, validity period and
  verification method; verification queue with pendency tracking. Resolves against the council
  appropriate to the candidate category through the A12 interfaces. Third-party verified checking is
  delivered in Part 2.
- **A2 · Two supply bases** — Base A and Base B as separate supply bases against a shared credential
  record, **not a status flag**.
- **A9 · Specialty and acuity capture** — Per-category attribute set on both requisition and
  candidate record. Requisition-side ownership is open item 1.
- **A12 · Configurable market layer** — Adapter interfaces behind identity, certification and
  qualification, selected by market and by candidate category. The India adapter set is in scope and
  covers the nursing councils, the NMC and state medical councils, the allied health councils and
  the Pharmacy Council. A further category is added by writing an adapter behind the same interface,
  not by changing core logic. **Must accept the US, Germany and Ireland later.**

### Messaging and engagement

- **D10 · Notifications and alerts** — Template management, trigger configuration, delivery status,
  retry, opt-out, preference management, per-user notification log.
- **D29 · Reminders, nudges and preferences** — Scheduling layer the cadence engine executes through.
- **A3 · Availability decay** — Availability confidence value decaying on a configurable schedule
  per base; reset on confirmed response.
- **A4 · Re-contact cadence engine** — Selects who to contact on confidence, configurable per
  segment. Base B runs every 3–4 days across the whole base.
- **A5 · Outbound voice with response capture** — Integration and response extraction schema. Voice
  provider not named.
- **A14 · Candidate response capture** — Consumes the A1 inbound stack with a candidate-response
  intent schema. Structured payload across WhatsApp template and app.

### Matching, selection, offer and placement

- **D5 · Matching and shortlisting** — Advanced search, saved searches, talent segments, recruiter
  override. Whether hand-assembly survives is open.
- **D18 · Matching and shortlisting (AI)** — Ranked set with rationale and unmet criteria, weighted
  per category and supply base. Weight model not specified.
- **A8 · Outcome write-back to ranking** — Selection outcomes and rejection reasons feed subsequent
  ranking. **Schema must be agreed with D6 before either is built.**
- **D6 · Interview and selection** — Slot proposal, reschedule, feedback, accept/hold/reject with
  mandatory structured rejection reasons and audit trail.
- **D20 · Offer management** — Offer generation, delivery, tracking, expiry, digital signature,
  offer-to-contract mapping. Without it the permanent chain stops after selection.
- **A13 · Client link surfaces** — One tokenised mechanism, four link types: shortlist response,
  day 30/60/90 check-in, timesheet approval. **Only day-90 is a billing trigger.** Carries client
  feedback as yes, no or with comments, verification of details, and configured reminders warning of
  auto-accept after the client waiting period.
- **D7 · Onboarding and joining** — Joining confirmation with date tracking; drop-out capture with
  reason returning the position to fulfilment.
- **A6 · Trial period tracking** — Check-ins at day 0, 7, 30, 60 and 90 capturing attendance, salary
  timeliness, preceptor and accommodation. Question sets differ by candidate category and by care
  setting, home healthcare having neither preceptor nor accommodation.

### Attendance, billing and analytics

- **D22 (thin) · Time and attendance** — Shift attendance by app check-in with a location stamp, and
  geo-fencing to set whether a check-in outside the boundary is allowed, warned or blocked. The
  boundary is the site for facility work and the visit address for home visits. Timesheet with
  client approval state. **No leave or overtime treatment.**
- **D22 (full) · Time and attendance — remainder** — Leave categorisation, overtime treatment and
  internal timesheet approval routing.
- **D8 · Billing and receivables** — Permanent placement invoice fired on the day-90
  client-acknowledged retention record, against contract terms in force.
- **A7 · Retention-linked invoicing** — The trigger record held as auditable evidence capable of
  supporting a billing query.
- **D25 (thin) · Billing — auto-invoice** — Shift-level invoice from the client-approved timesheet
  and contract. **No debit or credit notes, no dunning.**
- **D25 (full) · Billing — remainder and collections** — Payment receipt, reconciliation including
  part-payments and TDS, ageing, reminders and dunning, credit and debit notes.
- **D9 · Intelligence and analytics** — Funnel and conversion, fill rate, time-to-fill, trial
  survival.
- **D28 · Analytics — performance and MIS** — Stage-level conversion loss by client and recruiter,
  channel and recruiter performance, monthly MIS pack. Requires the D14 owner field.
- **A11 · Rate variance instrumentation** — Rate agreed against baseline by client, role, specialty
  and acuity.

### Later capability, migration and delivery

- **D12 · Client portal** — Logged-in website for clients: raise vacancies, track them, review
  shortlists, approve timesheets, download invoices. Becomes the sixth A1 intake channel.
- **D21 · Associate onboarding** — Associate profile, statutory forms including PF/UAN transfer and
  gratuity nomination, document checklist, app-guided onboarding.
- **D23 · Workforce operations** — Maker-checker request engine, attendance upload and verification
  pairing, salary revision individual and bulk, advance, hold and release.
- **D24 · Payroll** — Third-party engine integration, payroll inputs and cycle configuration, PF and
  ESIC reports and challans, payslips in-app and over WhatsApp.
- **A10 · Legacy migration (CPAL)** — Migration of CPAL and current tooling so the platform becomes
  system of record. **Effort basis awaits joint data profiling.**
- **D11 · Foundations and documentation** — Deployment to the client-hosted AWS Mumbai account,
  technical and user documentation, UAT support and go-live. Access control, environments and audit
  are delivered once under the foundation deliverables rather than separately here.
- **D30 · Foundations and documentation (R2)** — The Release 2 half of D11: documentation,
  acceptance testing and cutover for everything released after the first go-live.
