## Purpose

> Introduction to the feature. **This is not the scope** — the scope is items 1 to 5 below.

Interviews, the decision, and the offer — arranged between a candidate who has an app and a client
who has **no login at all**. Feedback is structured, and the decision carries **a mandatory reason
from a configured taxonomy**, because that taxonomy is what later feeds ranking. If accepted, the
offer is generated from a template, delivered, tracked to expiry, accepted, and **mapped to the
contract version whose terms billing will read at day 90**. Without this feature the permanent chain
stops after selection: no offer, no joining, no trial, no retention link, no invoice.

## Release in this phase

1. Interview slot proposal and confirmation.
2. Structured interview feedback.
3. Accept, hold or reject with a mandatory reason.
4. Offer generation from a template, issue and tracking.
5. Offer acceptance and offer-to-contract mapping.

## 1. Interview slot proposal and confirmation

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/interviews`

### Scenarios to cover

A recruiter proposes slots to a candidate and to the client. The candidate answers in the app; the
client answers on the channel they already use. The interview list shows **what is awaiting
feedback**, so a pipeline does not stall silently while everyone assumes someone else is chasing.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Interview | Record | Yes | Requisition, candidate, client, mode, location |
| 2 | Proposed slots | Repeating block | Yes | Date and time options |
| 3 | Candidate response | Action | — | Accept a slot or decline, in the app |
| 4 | Client confirmation | Action | — | Recorded by the recruiter in Phase 1 |
| 5 | Confirmed slot | Derived | — | Written when both sides agree |
| 6 | Interview list | Screen | — | Awaiting response, confirmed, held, awaiting feedback |
| 7 | Cancellation | Action | — | With a reason |

### Flow

1. The recruiter creates the interview and proposes slots.
2. The candidate accepts or declines in the app; the recruiter records the client's confirmation.
3. A confirmed interview moves to *awaiting feedback* after its slot passes.
4. A cancellation records a reason and returns the candidate to the shortlist.

### Scenarios to test

- An interview cannot be confirmed without a slot both sides accepted.
- Declining every slot returns the interview to the recruiter with the candidate still shortlisted.
- An interview past its slot with no feedback appears in *awaiting feedback* rather than disappearing.
- Cancellation without a reason is refused.

## 2. Structured interview feedback

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/interviews`

### Scenarios to cover

Feedback is captured in a structure that can be read by a machine later, not only as free text — it
is what the outcome write-back in Phase 3 will consume, and the shape has to exist from the start.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Feedback record | Record | Yes | Interview, author, submitted at |
| 2 | Criterion ratings | Structured | Yes | Per the configured criteria for the category |
| 3 | Overall recommendation | Single select | Yes | |
| 4 | Comments | Text | No | |
| 5 | Attachments | Files | No | Through the shared file store |

### Flow

1. The recruiter or interviewer records feedback against the configured criteria.
2. Feedback attaches to the interview and is visible on the candidate and the requisition.
3. The decision in item 3 reads it.

### Scenarios to test

- Feedback cannot be submitted without the mandatory ratings.
- Criteria change with the candidate category, from reference data.
- Feedback is immutable after submission except by a superseding entry.

## 3. Accept, hold or reject with a mandatory reason

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/interviews`

### Scenarios to cover

The decision, with a full audit trail and **a mandatory structured reason on a rejection**, taken
from a configured taxonomy. The taxonomy is agreed jointly with matching before either is built.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Decision | Accept / Hold / Reject | Yes | |
| 2 | Rejection reason | Reference | Yes on reject | From the configured taxonomy |
| 3 | Hold reason and review date | Fields | Yes on hold | |
| 4 | Decision record | Append-only | Yes | Actor, decision, reason, timestamp |
| 5 | Downstream effect | Behaviour | — | Accept opens the offer path; reject returns the position |

### Flow

1. The decision is recorded against the interview and the application.
2. A rejection records its structured reason; a hold records a review date.
3. An acceptance makes the offer path available for that candidate and requisition.
4. Every decision writes an audit entry and an event.

### Scenarios to test

- A rejection without a taxonomy reason is refused; free text alone is not enough.
- A decision cannot be edited — a change is a new, superseding decision.
- An acceptance is the only route to an offer.
- The reason taxonomy is reference data and changes without a deploy.

## 4. Offer generation, issue and tracking

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/offers`

### Scenarios to cover

The offer is generated from a template, previewed, issued to the candidate, and tracked to
acceptance, decline or expiry — with reminders on the way.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Offer | Record | Yes | Candidate, requisition, client, salary, joining date |
| 2 | Template | Configuration | Yes | **One template in Phase 1** |
| 3 | Variables | Resolution | Yes | From candidate, requisition, client and contract |
| 4 | Preview | Screen | Yes | What the candidate will receive, before issue |
| 5 | Issue | Action | Yes | Delivered through the sending path and visible in the app |
| 6 | Expiry | Date rule | Yes | With reminders before it |
| 7 | Status | Derived | Yes | Draft, issued, accepted, declined, expired |
| 8 | Decline reason | Reference | Yes on decline | |
| 9 | Offer versions | Records | — | A revised offer is a new version |

### Flow

1. An accepted decision opens offer creation; variables resolve from the records.
2. The recruiter previews and issues; the candidate receives it in the app and on their channel.
3. Reminders fire before expiry; expiry closes the offer with a status.
4. A revision issues a new version rather than editing the issued one.

### Scenarios to test

- An offer cannot be issued without a decision of accept.
- Preview and issued document are identical.
- Expiry fires exactly once and moves the status.
- A decline records a reason and returns the position to the requisition.
- A revised offer supersedes the previous version, which stays readable.

## 5. Offer acceptance and offer-to-contract mapping

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/offers`

### Scenarios to cover

Acceptance is what starts everything after it, and the offer is mapped to the **contract version**
whose terms — the placement fee in particular — billing will read at day 90. Getting this mapping
wrong is not visible for ninety days, which is why it is recorded at acceptance rather than derived
at invoicing.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Acceptance | Action | Yes | In the app, or recorded by the recruiter |
| 2 | Accepted at | Timestamp | Yes | |
| 3 | Contract version mapping | Reference | Yes | Resolved through *terms in force* at acceptance |
| 4 | Placement fee captured | Value | Yes | Snapshotted with its source version id |
| 5 | Handover to placement | Event | Yes | Opens joining confirmation |

### Flow

1. The candidate accepts; acceptance is recorded with its timestamp.
2. Terms in force resolve the contract version, and the offer records the version id and the fee.
3. The acceptance event opens joining confirmation in
   [placement and trial](/docs/release-plan/part-1/placement-and-trial).

### Scenarios to test

- Acceptance with no contract version in force is refused with the reason, and no placement opens.
- The recorded fee and version id still resolve a year later.
- Acceptance produces exactly one placement handover event, even if retried.
- An expired offer cannot be accepted.
