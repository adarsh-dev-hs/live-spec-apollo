## Purpose

> Introduction to the feature. **This is not the scope** — the scope is items 1 to 5 below.

Once someone accepts an offer, this feature tracks them joining and then checks in on them at **day
0, 7, 30, 60 and 90**. The candidate is asked whether they are being paid on time, whether they have
a preceptor and whether their accommodation is sorted. At day 90 the client is asked whether the
person is still there — and **that answer is what lets Apollo invoice**. The record it produces is
the most consequential object in the platform: it must still make sense to someone querying a charge
a year later, so it is immutable, it snapshots the terms in force, and it records whether the client
acknowledged or the window simply expired.

## Release in this phase

1. Joining confirmation and opening the placement.
2. Drop-out capture, returning the position to the requisition.
3. The trial check-in schedule for the nurse category in the hospital care setting.
4. Candidate check-in answers and the trial dashboard.
5. The immutable day-90 retention record.

## 1. Joining confirmation and opening the placement

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/placements` · `/placements/:id`

### Scenarios to cover

An accepted offer produces a placement when the candidate actually joins. Joining is confirmed with
a date, the placement opens, and **the 90-day clock starts from that date** — not from acceptance and
not from the offer.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Placement | Record | Yes | Candidate, requisition, client, site, offer, contract version |
| 2 | Joining date | Date | Yes | The clock start |
| 3 | Confirmation | Action | Yes | With who confirmed and when |
| 4 | Expected vs actual joining | Fields | Yes | The gap is visible, not silently overwritten |
| 5 | Placement status | Enum | Yes | Offered, joined, in trial, trial survived, trial ended |
| 6 | Placement list | Screen | — | With trial stage and billing-trigger visibility for finance |

### Flow

1. Offer acceptance hands over to placement, which awaits joining confirmation.
2. Confirming joining sets the date, opens the placement and schedules the trial events in item 3.
3. The requisition's filled count increases, moving it to partially filled or filled.
4. Finance can see, from the placement list, which placements are approaching a billing trigger.

### Scenarios to test

- The 90-day schedule is computed from the actual joining date.
- Confirming joining twice does not create two placements or two schedules.
- The requisition count updates on confirmation and reverses on a drop-out.
- The contract version recorded on the offer is carried onto the placement.

## 2. Drop-out capture

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/placements/:id`

### Scenarios to cover

Someone accepts and does not join, or joins and leaves inside the trial. The reason is recorded, the
placement ends, the trial schedule stops, and **the position returns to the requisition** so the
vacancy is worked again rather than quietly lost.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Drop-out | Action | — | Available before and during the trial |
| 2 | Reason | Reference | Yes | From the configured drop-out reason list |
| 3 | Effective date | Date | Yes | |
| 4 | Position return | Behaviour | Yes | The requisition reopens the position |
| 5 | Schedule cancellation | Behaviour | Yes | Remaining trial events and links are cancelled |

### Flow

1. A drop-out is recorded with a reason and a date.
2. The placement moves to trial ended; remaining check-ins and any pending links are cancelled.
3. The requisition returns to fulfilment with the position open again.
4. **No retention record is written and no invoice is raised.**

### Scenarios to test

- A drop-out without a reason is refused.
- Pending day-30, 60 and 90 activity is cancelled and no link is later sent.
- The requisition reopens the position and the board reflects it.
- A dropped-out placement can never produce a retention record.

## 3. The trial check-in schedule — nurse, hospital

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/trial` · `/admin/settings`

### Scenarios to cover

Check-ins are scheduled at **day 0, 7, 30, 60 and 90** from the joining date. Which events exist and
which questions each asks is **configuration per candidate category and per care setting** — Phase 1
configures the nurse category in the hospital care setting, and the configurability is what makes
Phase 2's other combinations a settings change rather than a build.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Check-in event definition | Configuration | Yes | Day offset, audience, question set |
| 2 | Question set | Reference data | Yes | Attendance, salary timeliness, preceptor, accommodation |
| 3 | Category and care-setting binding | Configuration | Yes | Phase 1: nurse · hospital |
| 4 | Schedule | Generated records | Yes | Five trial events per placement |
| 5 | Scheduler | Job | Yes | Runs on the job runtime |
| 6 | Rescheduling | Behaviour | Yes | A changed joining date moves unfired events only |

### Flow

1. Joining confirmation generates the five trial events from the configured definition.
2. The scheduler fires each at its day offset and dispatches the question set to its audience.
3. Day 90's client audience is served by the retention link in
   [client link surfaces](/docs/release-plan/part-1/client-link-surfaces).
4. Changing the joining date moves events that have not fired.

### Scenarios to test

- Five events are generated per placement, at the right offsets from the joining date.
- An event fires once, even if the scheduler runs twice.
- Adding a question to the set changes what is asked without a deploy.
- Moving the joining date moves unfired events and leaves fired ones untouched.

## 4. Candidate check-in answers and the trial dashboard

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/trial` · `/placements/:id`

### Scenarios to cover

The candidate is asked the configured questions and their answers are recorded against the trial
event. The trial dashboard shows **which check-ins are done, due or missed** across live placements,
so the trial is worked rather than watched.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Question rendering | App and message | Yes | The same set, whichever surface asks |
| 2 | Trial response | Record | Yes | Event, respondent, answers, received at |
| 3 | Negative answer flag | Derived | Yes | A negative answer is visible on the dashboard |
| 4 | Trial dashboard | Screen | — | Done, due, missed, by placement |
| 5 | Missed check-in | Derived | Yes | Not answered by the next event |
| 6 | Internal follow-up | Manual action | — | A recruiter records what they did |

### Flow

1. A fired event asks the candidate its questions through the sending path and the app.
2. Answers are recorded structured against the event.
3. The dashboard shows status per placement and per event, with negatives visible.
4. A recruiter follows up manually and records the follow-up.

### Scenarios to test

- An answer records against the correct event and placement.
- A missed check-in shows as missed rather than as pending forever.
- A negative answer is visible on the dashboard without a report being run.
- Answers are readable from the placement record.

## 5. The immutable day-90 retention record

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added* · **Delivers** `A7` (the record half)

**Screens** — `/placements/:id` · `/billing/evidence`

### Scenarios to cover

At day 90 the client either confirms the person is still there or the window expires. Either way a
**retention record** is written: immutable, snapshotting the contract terms in force, and recording
which of the two happened. This record is the input to the permanent placement invoice and the answer
to a billing query a year later.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Retention record | Append-only record | Yes | **No update, no delete** |
| 2 | Outcome | Enum | Yes | Client acknowledged / window expired |
| 3 | Responder | Reference | Yes when acknowledged | Which client contact answered, and when |
| 4 | Link reference | Reference | Yes | The link that produced the outcome |
| 5 | Contract version snapshot | Values | Yes | Terms in force at the trigger moment, with the version id |
| 6 | Placement snapshot | Values | Yes | Candidate, client, site, role, joining date |
| 7 | Billing trigger | Event | Yes | Handed to billing exactly once |

### Flow

1. The day-90 link answers, or its window expires.
2. Terms in force are resolved at that moment; **if nothing is in force the trigger does not fire**,
   and the placement is flagged for finance rather than silently invoiced.
3. The retention record is written with its snapshot and its outcome.
4. The billing trigger is raised once, idempotently, to
   [billing and receivables](/docs/release-plan/part-1/billing-and-receivables).

### Scenarios to test

- The record cannot be updated or deleted through any path, including the database role.
- Both outcomes produce a record, and each is distinguishable a year later.
- With no contract terms in force, no record is written, no invoice is raised, and finance sees why.
- A retried trigger produces one billing trigger, not two.
- The snapshot resolves without reading current contract data.
