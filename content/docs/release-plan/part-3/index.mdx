This document is meant for the engineering team, with complete clarity on what needs to be built for
**Phase 3**. Every page in this section is focused **only** on Phase 3, and no additional information
is provided. What was built earlier is specified in the [Phase 1 spec](/docs/release-plan/part-1) and
the [Phase 2 spec](/docs/release-plan/part-2), and is not repeated here.

**Due: end of Quarter 3.** Full depth. Phases 1 and 2 made every thread work. Phase 3 takes each of
them to its full width, moves Apollo's existing data onto the platform, and turns the platform into
the **system of record**.

<Callout type="warn" title="Payroll work starts in Quarter 1 even though payroll goes live in Quarter 3">
It integrates a third-party engine whose selection, contract and field mapping are all client
dependencies, and it sits behind associate onboarding, attendance and workforce operations. Starting
it in Quarter 3 would put it behind three predecessors with no room left. The **build** is specified
in [Payroll](/docs/release-plan/part-3/payroll); the **engine selection and mapping** begin in
Quarter 1.
</Callout>

## 5. The item with the least known about it

`A10` moves Apollo's candidate data out of CPAL, after which the old tool is switched off. **Its
effort basis awaits joint data profiling**, which has not happened: nobody has seen the volume, the
completeness, the duplicate rate, or whether fields map cleanly.

Profiling is the first piece of work on it and **it belongs early** — nothing else about `A10` can be
sized until it has run, and its result affects the Phase 3 plan more than any other single input. It
is item 1 of [legacy migration](/docs/release-plan/part-3/legacy-migration).

## 6. Phase 3 is done when

1. A requisition arrives on all five live channels plus verbal capture, and the six produce the same
   record.
2. Every report in `D9` and `D28` covers the whole period since the first requisition — not since
   the reports were built.
3. A pay run completes end to end through the third-party engine, with PF and ESIC reports produced
   and payslips delivered in the app and over WhatsApp.
4. A salary revision needs two people, and neither of them can be the same person.
5. A payment is received, matched across invoices with part-payment and TDS handled, and ageing runs
   off the contract payment terms rather than a flat rule.
6. The migrated base reconciles against the source total, duplicates against app registrations are
   resolved, and CPAL is switched off.
7. Documentation, UAT and cutover are complete for everything released after the first go-live.
