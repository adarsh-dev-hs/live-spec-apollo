## Purpose

> Introduction to the feature. **This is not the scope** — the scope is items 1 to 5 below.

The money coming back in. Billing ends at *invoice raised, receivable open*; this feature records
whether Apollo actually got paid. Its hard parts are not the happy path: a payment covering several
invoices partly, TDS deducted at source so gross and net both have to be visible, and ageing that
must run off **the contract's payment terms rather than a flat rule**.

## Release in this phase

1. Payments received and matched.
2. TDS recorded separately.
3. Ageing and the overdue report.
4. Chase history and automatic dunning.
5. Credit and debit notes.

## 1. Payments received and matched

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/receivables`

### Scenarios to cover

A client pays. The payment is recorded and matched across one or more invoices, including
**part-payments**, and anything that cannot be matched is queued rather than guessed at.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Payment | Record | Yes | Client, amount, date, reference, method |
| 2 | Allocation | Records | Yes | Payment split across invoices |
| 3 | Part-payment | Behaviour | Yes | An invoice may be partly settled |
| 4 | Unmatched queue | Screen | Yes | Payments with no confident allocation |
| 5 | Manual allocation | Action | Yes | With a reason, audited |
| 6 | Balance | Derived | Yes | Per invoice and per client |

### Flow

1. A payment is recorded, manually or from a finance import.
2. Matching proposes allocations; a person confirms or adjusts.
3. Balances update per invoice; unmatched amounts queue.

### Scenarios to test

- One payment across three invoices allocates to all three, to the decimal.
- A part-payment leaves the invoice open with the right balance.
- An unmatched payment queues and never auto-allocates on a guess.
- Allocation totals never exceed the payment amount.

## 2. TDS recorded separately

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/receivables` · `/invoices/:id`

### Scenarios to cover

Tax deducted at source means the amount received is less than the amount invoiced, and the invoice is
still fully settled. **Gross and net are both visible**, and a settled-with-TDS invoice is not chased.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | TDS amount | Field | Yes | On the payment allocation |
| 2 | Gross and net | Derived | Yes | Both displayed on the invoice |
| 3 | Settlement rule | Behaviour | Yes | Net received plus TDS closes the invoice |
| 4 | TDS report | Output | Yes | By client and period |

### Flow

1. A payment records its TDS component against each allocation.
2. Settlement compares net plus TDS against the invoice total.
3. The invoice closes when fully settled.

### Scenarios to test

- An invoice paid net of TDS closes and is not chased.
- Gross and net both display correctly and reconcile.
- TDS totals by client and period reconcile with the allocations.
- A TDS amount cannot exceed the invoice total.

## 3. Ageing and the overdue report

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/receivables`

### Scenarios to cover

Overdue is calculated **from the contract payment terms**, not from a flat thirty days, because
different clients agreed different terms and chasing on the wrong date damages the relationship.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Due date | Derived | Yes | From the contract version's payment terms |
| 2 | Ageing bands | Configuration | Yes | The agreed bands |
| 3 | Overdue report | Screen | — | By client, band, invoice type |
| 4 | Business dates | Behaviour | Yes | `Asia/Kolkata`, with the agreed holiday handling |
| 5 | Export | Action | — | For the finance pack |

### Flow

1. Each receivable takes its due date from the contract terms in force at invoicing.
2. Ageing bands are computed daily.
3. The overdue report drives the chase in item 4.

### Scenarios to test

- Two clients with different terms age differently on the same invoice date.
- A settled invoice leaves the overdue report immediately.
- Band boundaries are inclusive exactly as agreed.
- Export matches the on-screen filter.

## 4. Chase history and automatic dunning

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/receivables` · `/messaging/delivery-log`

### Scenarios to cover

Chasing on a schedule, **stopping the moment payment arrives**, and with a history so a client is not
chased twice by two people for the same invoice.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dunning schedule | Configuration | Yes | Steps, timing, template, recipient |
| 2 | Chase record | Record | Yes | Invoice, step, channel, sent at, outcome |
| 3 | Stop rule | Behaviour | Yes | Payment or credit note halts the schedule immediately |
| 4 | Manual chase | Action | Yes | Recorded on the same history |
| 5 | Escalation | Configuration | Yes | To an internal owner after the last step |

### Flow

1. An overdue receivable enters its dunning schedule.
2. Each step sends through the notification layer and records the chase.
3. Payment, part-settlement to zero, or a credit note stops the schedule.

### Scenarios to test

- A payment arriving mid-schedule stops the next step, verified at the boundary.
- A manual chase appears on the same history as automatic ones.
- No invoice receives two steps on the same day from two sources.
- Escalation fires once, after the final step.

## 5. Credit and debit notes

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/invoices` · `/invoices/:id`

### Scenarios to cover

An invoice was wrong, or a further charge is due. A note is raised, **approved before issue**, and
exported alongside invoices — the invoice itself is never silently edited.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Credit note | Record | Yes | Against an invoice, with lines and a reason |
| 2 | Debit note | Record | Yes | Same shape, opposite direction |
| 3 | Approval | Gate | Yes | **Approved before issue**, through maker-checker |
| 4 | Numbering | Sequence | Yes | Its own gapless sequence |
| 5 | Effect on balance | Behaviour | Yes | Adjusts the receivable, not the invoice record |
| 6 | Export | Action | — | Alongside invoices for the period |

### Flow

1. A note is raised against an invoice with lines and a reason.
2. It is approved, issued, and the document is generated and stored.
3. The receivable balance adjusts; the original invoice is unchanged.

### Scenarios to test

- An unapproved note cannot be issued.
- The original invoice is never edited by a note.
- The receivable balance reflects the note exactly.
- Note numbering is gapless and separate from invoice numbering.
- A credit note taking a balance to zero stops dunning.
