## Purpose

> Introduction to the feature. **This is not the scope** — the scope is items 1 to 3 below.

The profile and the statutory forms already exist. Phase 3 adds what makes them work at volume: a
checklist that differs by role and by state, an app flow that walks the associate through it, and
**the gate that blocks a pay run for an associate whose paperwork is incomplete**. That gate is the
operational reason the feature exists.

## Release in this phase

1. The document checklist by role and state.
2. The app-guided onboarding flow.
3. The pay-run gate.

## 1. The document checklist

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/workforce/onboarding`

### Scenarios to cover

What an associate must provide **differs by role and by state**. The checklist is generated from
configuration, tracked per document, and shows exactly what is outstanding.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Checklist definition | Configuration | Yes | Documents required, by role and state |
| 2 | Checklist instance | Record | Yes | Per associate, generated on onboarding |
| 3 | Item state | Enum | Yes | Outstanding, submitted, accepted, returned |
| 4 | Review | Action | Yes | Accept or return with a reason |
| 5 | Completeness | Derived | Yes | Feeds the gate in item 3 |

### Flow

1. Onboarding generates the checklist from the associate's role and state.
2. Documents are submitted through the app or the console and reviewed.
3. Completeness updates as items are accepted.

### Scenarios to test

- Two associates in different states get different checklists.
- A returned item states its reason to the associate.
- Completeness reflects accepted items only, not submitted ones.
- Changing a definition does not invalidate an already-complete associate retrospectively.

## 2. The app-guided onboarding flow

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/app/onboarding`

### Scenarios to cover

The associate completes onboarding on their phone: what is needed, what is done, what is left, and
where to fix something that was returned — without an operations user chasing each step by message.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Guided steps | Screens | Yes | Profile, forms, documents, in order |
| 2 | Progress indicator | Component | Yes | Steps done and remaining |
| 3 | Capture | Component | Yes | Camera and file, through the shared upload control |
| 4 | Returned-item handling | Behaviour | Yes | Reason shown, resubmission in place |
| 5 | Reminders | Triggers | Yes | Through the reminder layer, stopping on completion |

### Flow

1. The associate is invited into the flow and works through the steps.
2. Submissions are reviewed; returns come back with a reason.
3. Reminders continue until the checklist completes, then stop.

### Scenarios to test

- The flow reflects the associate's own checklist, not a generic one.
- A returned item is resubmitted in place, with its history retained.
- Reminders stop the moment onboarding completes.
- The flow works on a poor connection, queueing uploads.

## 3. The pay-run gate

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/workforce/onboarding` · `/workforce/payroll`

### Scenarios to cover

**An associate with incomplete paperwork cannot be included in a pay run.** The gate is enforced in
the pay run itself, the exclusion is visible with its reason, and the operations team can see exactly
what is missing.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gate rule | Configuration | Yes | Which checklist items and forms are blocking |
| 2 | Gate evaluation | Behaviour | Yes | At input lock in the pay run |
| 3 | Exclusion record | Record | Yes | Associate, cycle, reason |
| 4 | Blocking-items view | Screen | Yes | What is missing, per excluded associate |
| 5 | Override | Action | Yes | Only through the maker-checker engine, with a reason |

### Flow

1. Locking a pay cycle evaluates the gate for every associate in it.
2. Blocked associates are excluded with their missing items listed.
3. An override, if permitted, goes through maker-checker and is recorded.

### Scenarios to test

- An associate missing a blocking item is excluded from the run.
- The exclusion names exactly what is missing.
- An override cannot be applied by one person alone.
- Completing the paperwork includes the associate in the next cycle without manual intervention.
