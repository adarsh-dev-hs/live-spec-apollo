## Purpose

> Introduction to the feature. **This is not the scope** — the scope is items 1 to 4 below.

Everything the contractual chain needs from the candidate happens in the app. **Shift attendance is
captured by app check-in with no alternative route: no app, no attendance, no timesheet, no shift
invoice.** That makes this the single most load-bearing feature in Phase 2, and it is why the store
submission belongs in Phase 1 rather than here.

## Release in this phase

1. Shift discovery.
2. Shift acceptance with clash prevention.
3. Check-in and check-out with an offline queue.
4. The in-app availability answer.

## 1. Shift discovery

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/app/shifts` · `/app/home`

### Scenarios to cover

A Base B candidate opens the app and sees shifts they can actually work: their category, their
location, within travel, and not clashing with something they have already accepted.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shift list | Screen | — | Date, time band, site or area, role, rate where shown |
| 2 | Eligibility filter | Behaviour | Yes | Category, credentials, base membership, location |
| 3 | Shift detail | Screen | — | Full detail including the address rule for home care |
| 4 | Sort and filter | Controls | — | By date, distance, time band |
| 5 | Ranking input | Behaviour | Yes | The ranked position from matching informs what is surfaced |

### Flow

1. Open shift demand lines are matched against the candidate's eligibility.
2. The list surfaces them, ordered by the ranking and the candidate's filters.
3. Opening a shift shows its full detail before acceptance.

### Scenarios to test

- A candidate without the required credential does not see the shift.
- A Base A-only candidate sees no shifts.
- A filled line disappears from discovery immediately.
- Home care shifts show the area, and the exact address only when acceptance requires it.

## 2. Shift acceptance with clash prevention

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/app/shifts`

### Scenarios to cover

The candidate accepts a shift. Two people cannot take the last slot on one line, and one person
cannot accept two shifts that overlap — including travel where that is configured.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Accept | Action | — | Against a demand line |
| 2 | Quantity guard | Constraint | Yes | Enforced at the database, not only in the app |
| 3 | Clash rule | Behaviour | Yes | Overlapping time bands are refused with an explanation |
| 4 | Acceptance record | Record | Yes | Candidate, line, accepted at |
| 5 | Withdrawal | Action | Yes | Before a cut-off, with a reason; the line reopens |
| 6 | Confirmation | Message | Yes | Through the notification layer |

### Flow

1. Acceptance is attempted against the line and guarded by the remaining quantity.
2. A clash with an existing acceptance is refused with the clashing shift named.
3. A successful acceptance reduces the line's remaining quantity and confirms to the candidate.
4. Withdrawal before the cut-off reopens the slot and notifies the recruiter.

### Scenarios to test

- Two candidates accepting the last slot concurrently produce one acceptance and one clear refusal.
- An overlapping acceptance is refused and names the clash.
- Withdrawal reopens the slot and appears on the board.
- A withdrawn shift cannot later be checked into.

## 3. Check-in and check-out with an offline queue

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/app/check-in`

### Scenarios to cover

The candidate checks in at the start of a shift and out at the end, **with a location stamp**. A
hospital basement with no signal must not lose an attendance record: the check-in queues locally,
carries its true timestamp and location, and syncs when the device reconnects.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Check-in | Action | Yes | Against an accepted shift, with a location stamp |
| 2 | Check-out | Action | Yes | Same, at the end of the shift |
| 3 | Location capture | Capability | Yes | Coordinates and accuracy recorded |
| 4 | Geo-fence response | Behaviour | Yes | Allow, warn or block per configuration — the rule lives in [attendance](/docs/release-plan/part-2/attendance-and-timesheets) |
| 5 | Offline queue | Mechanism | Yes | Retains the **original** timestamp and location |
| 6 | Sync | Mechanism | Yes | Idempotent, ordered, with conflict reporting |
| 7 | Permission handling | Behaviour | Yes | Location denied is a recorded exception, not a silent pass |

### Flow

1. The candidate checks in; the app captures time, coordinates and accuracy.
2. The geo-fence rule for that site or visit address decides allow, warn or block.
3. Online, the record posts immediately; offline, it queues.
4. On reconnect the queue syncs idempotently; duplicates are collapsed and conflicts reported.

### Scenarios to test

- A check-in made offline arrives with its original time and location, not the sync time.
- The same queued check-in synced twice produces one attendance record.
- A blocked check-in outside the fence is refused with an explanation and recorded as an exception.
- Denied location permission produces a recorded exception a person can resolve.
- A check-out without a check-in is refused.

## 4. The in-app availability answer

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/app/availability`

### Scenarios to cover

The candidate says when they are available. The answer uses **the same structured schema as the
WhatsApp answer**, so an answer arriving on either channel reconciles to one record and resets the
same availability confidence.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Availability question set | Configuration | Yes | The same set the cadence engine asks |
| 2 | Answer capture | Screen | Yes | Rendered from the set |
| 3 | Shared response schema | Schema | Yes | Identical across app, WhatsApp and voice |
| 4 | Confidence reset | Behaviour | Yes | A confirmed answer resets the decay |
| 5 | Last-confirmed display | Indicator | — | The candidate sees when they last answered |

### Flow

1. The candidate opens availability and answers the configured questions.
2. The answer is written on the shared schema and reconciled by
   [engagement and availability](/docs/release-plan/part-2/engagement-and-availability).
3. Confidence resets and ranking sees a fresh candidate.

### Scenarios to test

- An app answer and a WhatsApp answer produce the same record shape.
- Answers on two channels within the window reconcile to one, without double-counting.
- A confirmed answer resets confidence immediately.
- Changing the question set changes what the app asks with no app release.
