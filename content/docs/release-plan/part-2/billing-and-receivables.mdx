## Purpose

> Introduction to the feature. **This is not the scope** — the scope is items 1 to 3 below.

The second invoice path. A **contractual engagement** — including a locum doctor paid per session —
invoices from a client-approved timesheet, where the permanent path invoices from a day-90 retention
record. **The trigger follows the service line, not the candidate category.** After this phase both
paths are visible in one place, each against the evidence that allowed it.

## Release in this phase

1. Shift invoice generation from an approved or deemed-approved timesheet.
2. Both invoice paths in one place, each with its evidence.
3. Duplicate prevention on the shift path.

[Phase 3 · Collections](/docs/release-plan/part-3/collections).

## 1. Shift invoice generation

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/invoices` · `/invoices/:id`

### Scenarios to cover

An approved or deemed-approved timesheet raises a shift invoice, at the prices already recorded on
the timesheet lines. Billing invoices from **the approved timesheet, never from raw attendance**.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Invoice | Record | Yes | Client, type (shift), period, currency, status |
| 2 | Invoice lines | Records | Yes | From the timesheet lines, with their rate version ids |
| 3 | Source timesheet | Reference | Yes | And its approval state |
| 4 | Contract version | Reference | Yes | Recorded, not resolved at render time |
| 5 | Invoice document | File | Yes | Generated and stored |
| 6 | Numbering | Sequence | Yes | The same gapless rule as the permanent path |
| 7 | Trigger | Event | Yes | Raised on approval or deemed approval |

### Flow

1. Approval or deemed approval raises the billing trigger.
2. Billing composes the invoice from the timesheet lines as priced.
3. The invoice document is generated, the invoice is raised and a receivable opens.

### Scenarios to test

- An invoice total equals the sum of its timesheet lines, to the decimal.
- A timesheet not approved or deemed approved cannot produce an invoice.
- A reissued timesheet produces a corrected invoice path rather than a silent edit.
- The recorded contract version resolves later, after newer versions exist.

## 2. Both invoice paths in one place

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/invoices` · `/billing/evidence` · `/receivables`

### Scenarios to cover

Finance works one list. A permanent invoice and a shift invoice sit side by side, each linked to the
evidence that allowed it — a retention record for one, an approved timesheet and its link history for
the other.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Unified invoice list | Screen | — | Filter by type, client, period, status |
| 2 | Evidence panel per type | Screen | Yes | Retention record, or timesheet and approval link |
| 3 | Receivable | Record | Yes | Opened by either path, aged from the contract terms |
| 4 | Type-specific detail | Rendering | Yes | Each type shows its own line structure |
| 5 | Export | Action | — | Across both types for a period |

### Flow

1. Both paths write into the same invoice and receivable structures.
2. The evidence panel renders the evidence appropriate to the invoice's type.
3. Receivables age from the contract payment terms, whichever path raised them.

### Scenarios to test

- Filtering by type separates the two cleanly, and the unfiltered total is their sum.
- A shift invoice's evidence reaches the timesheet, its approval link and the attendance behind it.
- A permanent invoice's evidence is unchanged from Phase 1.
- Ageing behaves identically for both types.

## 3. Duplicate prevention on the shift path

**ClickUp item** — *link to be added*

**Screens** — `/invoices`

### Scenarios to cover

One approved timesheet produces **one** invoice, whatever happens to the trigger — a retry, a
redelivered event, a re-queued dead letter, or an approval arriving twice.

### Elements

| # | Element | Type | Mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Idempotency key | Field | Yes | Derived from the timesheet and its version |
| 2 | Uniqueness constraint | Database | Yes | One invoice per timesheet version |
| 3 | Reissue handling | Behaviour | Yes | A superseding timesheet is a distinct key |
| 4 | Exception queue | Screen state | — | Triggers that could not invoice, with the reason |

### Flow

1. The trigger carries the timesheet version's idempotency key.
2. Creation is guarded at the database.
3. A repeat returns the existing invoice; a reissued timesheet is treated as a new basis.

### Scenarios to test

- The same approval delivered five times produces one invoice.
- A reissued timesheet does not silently duplicate the original invoice.
- Concurrent triggers produce one invoice, proven under load.
- An unpriceable timesheet lands in the exception queue with a reason.
