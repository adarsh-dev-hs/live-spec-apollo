## 2. The second chain

The contractual line of business runs alongside the permanent one and behaves differently at every
step. Part 2 is where it becomes real.

```
contract carries shift rates and a deemed-approval window
  → shift requisition and shift template  →  one demand line per shift
  → candidates ranked against the shift line, Base B weights
  → candidate accepts the shift in the app
  → candidate checks in and out in the app, with a location stamp and geo-fencing
  → attendance exceptions resolved  →  timesheet compiled and priced against the contract version
  → timesheet sent to the client on a tokenised link  →  approved, or deemed approved on expiry
  → shift invoice raised  →  receivable opened
```

**The app is the only route by which attendance reaches the platform.** No app, no attendance, no
timesheet, no shift invoice. That single dependency sets the sequence for the whole of Part 2.

## 6. Part 2 is done when

1. A requisition arrives by email, by WhatsApp and through the portal, and the three produce records
   differing only in source channel and raw payload.
2. A candidate accepts a shift in the app, checks in inside the geo-fence, and the shift reaches a
   timesheet.
3. A client approves that timesheet on a link — or the window expires and it is deemed approved —
   and a shift invoice is raised against the contract version in force.
4. A client user signs in to the portal and sees only their own sites and requisitions.
5. The cadence engine contacts Base B on its own schedule, over WhatsApp and voice, and an answer
   arriving on either channel reconciles to one record.
6. A credential is verified by a third party rather than by a person reading a document.
7. Doctors, allied health and home healthcare are configuration, not code.
