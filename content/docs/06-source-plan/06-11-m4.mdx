This holds everything about the healthcare professionals: nurses, doctors and allied health staff. There are two separate pools — one for permanent placements and one for shift work — but both share the same set of credential records. The module covers checking who someone is and that their registration is real (using the adapter layer from M1), a work queue for the verification team, and the candidate mobile app. The app is where candidates sign up with their phone number, upload a CV, find jobs and shifts, apply, and check in at the start of a shift.

### Built once, used elsewhere

The mobile app is used by three modules. It sits here, but shift discovery and shift check-in are how M8 gets its attendance data, and the app is one of two ways candidates answer availability questions in M5. Because shift check-in is the only way attendance reaches the system, no app means no attendance, which means no timesheet and no shift invoice. Plan the app against all three, not just this module. It also carries the data protection work - consent, residency and retention - which is first needed the moment candidate personal data is captured.

### Records this milestone owns

- Candidate — the person. Holds their category, which pool they are in, and their availability confidence score (M5 writes it, M6 reads it).
- Credential — a registration or qualification: who issued it, how long it is valid, and how it was checked. Shared across both pools.
- CandidateDocument — CVs, ID scans, certificates. The text pulled out by OCR is attached to these.
- VerificationCase — one check, with a green, amber or red result, and a note of how long it has been waiting.
- CategoryAttributeSet — the extra fields that differ by category: specialty and acuity for nurses; specialty, case-mix and grade for doctors; discipline, equipment and technique for allied health.
- Application, ShiftAcceptance — what the candidate has applied for or agreed to work.
- DuplicateCandidate — two records that look like the same person, waiting for someone to decide.

### How records move

- Candidate: registered → profile complete → verification pending → verified, partly verified, or failed
- Credential: claimed → submitted → being checked → verified (green), flagged (amber) or rejected (red) → expired
- VerificationCase: queued → in progress → resolved, waiting on an outside registry, or handed to a person to check manually
- Application: applied → shortlisted → interviewing → offered → joined. Or rejected, or withdrawn.

### Not in this milestone

- Bulk candidate loads from campuses or partners, walk-in registration and referral tagging. Removed from this contract; Apollo handles these by hand.
- Face recognition, biometrics and cheating detection in assessments. Later release.
- Blocking a shift check-in based on where the phone is. We record the location but do not act on it. Enforcing it is milestone M12.
- Statutory joining forms such as PF and gratuity nomination. That is milestone M11.
- Producing the clinical and communication rating. The field exists on the record but nothing fills it in — see open item OI-A-07.

### Needs to exist first

- M1 for the adapter layer — without it no identity or credential check can run. M1 also supplies the migrated candidate pool.

### What breaks without it

- M5 writes availability onto the candidate record. M6 ranks on credentials, category fields and availability, and cannot rank if any of the three is missing. M8 gets shift attendance from the app. M7 quotes the candidate on shortlists and offers.
