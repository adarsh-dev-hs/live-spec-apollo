Two separate jobs sit here. The first is billing: raise a permanent placement invoice once the client confirms at day 90 that the person is still there, and raise shift invoices from approved timesheets. Both must keep the evidence that triggered them, because Apollo may be asked months later to justify a charge. The second is reporting: how many vacancies get filled, how fast, where candidates drop out, how each recruiter and each intake channel performs, and how the rate agreed compares to the standard rate. These two halves share nothing except the underlying event data and can be staffed separately.

### Built once, used elsewhere

Nothing is shared between the billing engine and the reporting layer except the event data underneath. They can be built by different people at the same time.

### Records this milestone owns

- Invoice, InvoiceLine — two kinds: a permanent placement fee (5-6% of salary), and a shift invoice.
- BillingTrigger — the evidence that allowed the invoice: either the day-90 confirmation or the approved timesheet.
- Receivable — money owed against the invoice. This is where the process stops in this contract.
- ReportingEvent — events collected from every module and reshaped for reporting.
- RateBaseline, RateVariance — the standard rate against what was actually agreed.

### How records move

- BillingTrigger: pending → confirmed by the client, or automatically treated as confirmed after the waiting period → used by an invoice
- Invoice: draft → raised → sent to the finance system. A receivable opens.

### Not in this milestone

- Receiving payments, matching them off, part-payments, TDS, chasing overdue money, and credit or debit notes. All milestone M15.
- The finance system itself. Nobody has said which one it is, so we build a generic export it can attach to later.
- Forecasting demand. Not in this contract.
- Any client-facing dashboard. Clients only see the links from M7 until the portal in M10.

### Needs to exist first

- M8 for the day-90 record and the approved timesheet. M7 for the client link that captures the confirmation. M2 for the contract terms read at the moment of invoicing. M3 for the owner field the recruiter reports need. Events from every module.

### What breaks without it

- Nothing inside the platform. The chain ends at invoice raised and money owed recorded.
