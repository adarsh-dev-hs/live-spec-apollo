Once someone accepts an offer, this module tracks them joining and then checks in on them at day 0, 7, 30, 60 and 90. The candidate is asked whether they are being paid on time, whether they have a preceptor, and whether their accommodation is sorted. The client is asked at day 30 and 60 how it is going, and at day 90 whether the person is still there — that last answer is what lets Apollo invoice. Separately, for shift workers, this module collects attendance from the app check-ins and builds it into a timesheet, which the client then approves through a link.

### Built once, used elsewhere

The check-in questions differ by candidate category, so they use the same category setup as M4. The client-facing parts of the trial run on the link mechanism built in M7.

### Records this milestone owns

- Placement — the person in the job: joining date, which vacancy, which contract. The 90-day clock hangs off this.
- DropOut — someone who did not join or left early, with a reason. The position goes back to M3 as open.
- TrialEvent — the day 0, 7, 30, 60 and 90 check-ins, on both the candidate and client side.
- TrialResponse — the answers: attendance, whether pay arrived on time, preceptor, accommodation.
- RetentionRecord — the day-90 answer, or the automatic version if the client never replied. This is the evidence behind the invoice.
- ShiftAttendance — check-in and check-out, with where the phone was at the time (recorded, not enforced).
- Timesheet — a period of shifts, with a state showing whether the client has approved it.

### How records move

- Placement: joined → in trial → trial survived, or trial ended with a reason
- Placement: offered → dropped out with a reason (the position goes back to open in M3)
- TrialEvent: scheduled → due → answered, escalated to a phone call, or missed
- ShiftAttendance: expected → checked in → checked out. Or no show, or a problem raised → resolved.
- Timesheet: building → built (not yet approved) → sent for approval → approved, or automatically treated as approved after the waiting period → invoiced

### Not in this milestone

- Paying the nurses. Payroll runs outside the platform for this whole period — that is milestone M13.
- Requests and approvals for salary changes, advances and holds. Milestone M12.
- Joining paperwork and statutory forms. Milestone M11.
- Leave types, overtime rules and internal approval routing on timesheets. Milestone M15.
- Blocking a check-in based on location. We store the location but do not act on it. Milestone M12.

### Needs to exist first

- M7 for the accepted offer and the link mechanism. M4 for the app, which is the only way attendance arrives.

### What breaks without it

- M9 needs the day-90 record to raise a permanent invoice, and the approved timesheet to raise a shift invoice. M3 needs the fill and drop-out counts.
