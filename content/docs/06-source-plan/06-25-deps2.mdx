Everything the platform needs from outside the build team, and what specifically is required from Apollo for each. These are separate from the open items, which are decisions about what to build; these are accounts, contracts, registrations and access that must exist before a build can start or a supplier can be called.

> **Start these in week one.** Five things should start in week one regardless of when their milestone begins: Meta business verification for WhatsApp, DLT registration for SMS, the verification aggregator contract, the two app store accounts, and the decision on whether the console federates to Apollo's identity provider. The first four are pure waiting on someone else's timetable and no amount of delivery capacity shortens them. The fifth changes what gets built, so it is cheapest to answer before anything is built at all.

> Most of these are not technical work. They are accounts, contracts, registrations and decisions that have to exist before a build can start or a supplier can be called, and almost all of them need someone at Apollo rather than someone on the delivery team. The items marked as coming through Apollo's clients are the slowest, because they need Apollo to go and ask their own hospital clients, which is two hops rather than one.

**First needed for M0-I1 sign in.** This changes what M0 builds. Email and password is a small build; federating to a corporate identity provider is a different one, and retrofitting it after ninety users are live is worse than either. Decide before M0 starts.

##### Required from Apollo

- Decide whether the Ops and Admin console must federate to Apollo's identity provider
- If so, name the provider and supply the federation configuration

#### X01 — WhatsApp Business Platform

`Month 3` · `Messaging` · `Apollo`

**First needed for M3-I25 WhatsApp inbound and follow-up.** The longest lead time in the plan and the only one that is pure waiting. Business verification and template approval run on Meta's timetable. Templates are needed by M3 follow-ups, M5 contact schedules, M7 offers and links, and M8 check-ins, so the approval queue is long and each unapproved template blocks a flow.

##### Required from Apollo

- Meta Business verification in Apollo's legal name
- WhatsApp Business Account
- Verified sender number, and the display name approved
- Contract with a Business Solution Provider - Gupshup, Karix or Twilio
- Message template approvals, and re-approval on every wording change
- Confirmed per-conversation pricing so running cost can be modelled

##### Who has to act

Apollo

##### Track

#### X03 — Email sending domain

`Month 3` · `Messaging` · `Apollo IT`

**First needed for M3-I26 email inbound, M3-I31 message sending.** SES starts sandboxed and only sends to verified addresses until the production request is granted, which is a written request with a review. Missing DNS records send platform email to spam, which looks like a product failure rather than a configuration one.

##### Required from Apollo

- DNS records on the sending domain - SPF, DKIM and DMARC
- SES production access request, since new accounts start in sandbox
- The monitored mailbox or forwarding address for inbound requisitions

##### Who has to act

Apollo IT
