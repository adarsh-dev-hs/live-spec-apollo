*M11 · D21 · Months 5–6*

`Release 2` · `5 items` · `18 conditions` · `26.5pd` · `0 high risk` · `Candidate App, Ops/Admin`

The paperwork side of someone joining as a shift worker. Today this is collected and stored outside the platform. This milestone brings it in: an associate profile, the statutory forms including PF and UAN transfer and gratuity nomination, a checklist of documents to collect, and a guided step-by-step flow in the mobile app so the candidate can do it themselves.

### Built once, used elsewhere

The document upload and storage built for M4 credentials is reused here. The guided flow lives in the same mobile app.

### Records this milestone owns

- AssociateProfile — the extra employment details needed once someone is actually working, beyond their candidate record.
- StatutoryForm — PF and UAN transfer, gratuity nomination and the rest, with a completion state each.
- OnboardingChecklist — which documents are needed and which have arrived.

### How records move

- AssociateProfile: not started → in progress → complete → verified
- StatutoryForm: pending → submitted → verified. Or returned to the candidate with a reason.

### Not in this milestone

- Filing the statutory returns themselves. Filing is outside this contract entirely.
- Onboarding for permanent placements. Those join the client directly, not Apollo.

### Needs to exist first

- M4 for the candidate record and document storage. M8 for the placement that triggers onboarding.

### What breaks without it

- M13 payroll needs a complete associate profile and statutory forms before anyone can be paid.

#### M11-I1 — Associate details

`D21` · `Screen` · `L · 3.0pd` · `AI High` · `Medium` · `COMP` · `Low`

**Who uses it: Ops, Associate.** The extra employment details needed once a shift worker actually starts, beyond their candidate record.

##### Screens to build

1. **Associate details**
  - Takes the employment details needed once someone starts working
  - Takes bank details and statutory identifiers
  - Shows what is still missing

##### Conditions it must satisfy

- An associate profile builds on the candidate record rather than duplicating it
- Bank details are stored encrypted and access is restricted (M4-I27)
- A profile cannot be marked complete with mandatory statutory fields missing
- Payroll in M13 cannot run for an incomplete profile

#### M11-I2 — Statutory forms

`D21` · `Screen` · `L · 3.0pd` · `AI Medium` · `Medium` · `COMP` · `Low`

**Who uses it: Associate, Ops.** PF and UAN transfer, gratuity nomination and the rest, collected in the platform instead of on paper.

##### Screens to build

1. **Statutory forms**
  - Lists the forms the associate must complete
  - Takes each form, including PF and UAN transfer and gratuity nomination
  - Returns a form to the associate with a reason

##### Conditions it must satisfy

- Each form has its own completion state, not one overall flag
- A returned form tells the candidate exactly what to fix
- Completed forms are stored as documents and retained (M0-I6)
- Filing the forms with the authorities is not in scope

#### M11-I3 — Document checklist

`D21` · `Screen` · `M · 2.0pd` · `AI High` · `Low` · `XMOD` · `Low`

**Who uses it: Ops.** Which documents are needed for this associate, and which have arrived.

##### Screens to build

1. **Document checklist**
  - Lists which documents this associate must provide and which have arrived

#### M11-I4 — App: onboarding

`D21` · `Screen` · `L · 3.0pd` · `AI High` · `Medium` · `SHARED` · `Low`

**Who uses it: Associate.** A step-by-step flow in the mobile app so the associate completes onboarding themselves.

##### Screens to build

1. **App: onboarding**
  - Walks the associate through onboarding one task at a time
  - Takes photos of documents and fills in the forms
  - Saves progress so it can be finished later

##### Conditions it must satisfy

- An associate can complete onboarding entirely in the app without an Ops user
- Progress is saved so it can be finished across several sessions
- Guided flows live in the app, not WhatsApp (Part A convention)
- A rejected item reappears in the flow with the reason shown

`D21` · `Service` · `M · 2.0pd` · `AI Medium` · `Medium` · `XMOD` · `Low`

**Who uses it: System.** Stops payroll running for someone whose paperwork is not finished.
