This holds the client side of the business: the hospital or home-healthcare company, its sites and departments, the rates agreed with it, and the signed contract. Two things here matter more than they look. First, the list of email addresses and WhatsApp numbers belonging to each client — M3 uses it to work out who sent an incoming requisition. Second, the contract terms, because the billing engine in M9 reads them at the moment it decides to raise an invoice.

### Built once, used elsewhere

The sender list built here is what M3 checks every incoming message against. The client contact record is the most likely place to hold the people who receive the tokenised links in M7. It also carries the shared list component every Ops screen reuses, first needed here for the client and rate card lists.

### Records this milestone owns

- Client, Location, Department — created here. Every other module reads them and none of them writes back.
- ClientContact — the people at the client. Probably also where link recipients live (see open item OI-A-12).
- SenderIdentity — an email address, WhatsApp number or HRMS account, mapped to one client. M3 looks up every inbound message here.
- RateCard, RateCardLine — what we charge, by role, skill and location, with the dates each rate applies from.
- Contract, ContractVersion — the signed agreement and its terms. M9 reads this when it raises an invoice.
- ClientWindowConfig — three waiting periods per client: how long before we treat retention as confirmed, how long before we treat a timesheet as approved, and how long before we chase a missing requisition field.

### How records move

- Client: draft → active → suspended → closed
- Contract: draft → active → renewed (creates a new version) → expired. Or terminated, with a reason.
- RateCardVersion: draft → in effect → superseded. Old versions are never deleted, because an old requisition must still price against the rate that applied when it was raised.

### Not in this milestone

- Client login and self-service. That is the client portal, milestone M10.
- Writing contracts from a template. In this milestone contracts are recorded, not drafted.
- Credit limits and credit control. Billing stops at invoice raised.

### Needs to exist first

- M1 — logins, roles and a running environment.

### What breaks without it

- M3 cannot create a requisition without a client, and cannot identify an inbound sender without the sender list. M7 needs the contract to map an offer to. M9 cannot raise a permanent invoice at all without the contract terms — there is no fallback.

#### M2-I10 — Client waiting periods

`D13` · `Config` · `L · 3.0pd` · `AI High` · `High` · `SPEC` · `Low`

**Who uses it: BD, Finance, Platform administrator.** Three waiting periods per client, each with a platform-wide fallback so an unconfigured client never stalls invoicing.

##### Screens to build

1. **Client waiting periods**
  - Sets how many days before retention counts as confirmed if the client does not reply

- Apollo has not yet supplied the default values (OI-A-05)
