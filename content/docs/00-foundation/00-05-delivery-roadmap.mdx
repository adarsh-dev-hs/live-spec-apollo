## 2. Phase gates

The client requires a gate every three to four weeks with a **working demonstration**, not a document,
and named attendees: **Shallu Kakkar** (fulfilment and recruiter workflow), **Suman** (client-facing
surfaces), **Mr. Abednego Peter** (clinical quality and the candidate record). A written record at each
gate of what was raised and what changed.

| Gate | Week | Demonstration that passes it | Attendee |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | 4 | A named recruiter signs in, sees only their permitted navigation, an administrator adds a candidate category as **data**, and every action appears on the activity trail | Shallu |
| G2 | 8 | **One client, one nurse, one link.** A tokenised day-90 link is issued to a real hospital contact, opened on a phone, answered, and produces an immutable `RetentionRecord`. *This is the client's own request and it settles the single riskiest behavioural assumption in the plan.* | Suman |
| G2b | 8 | **A crude ranked list on three fields** against a live requisition, in front of recruiters. The overrides they make are the weighting specification (`OI-A-11`) | Shallu |
| G3 | 12 | The same requirement sent by email, spreadsheet, WhatsApp text and WhatsApp voice note produces four requisitions differing only in source channel and raw payload | Shallu |
| G4 | 16 | A Base B nurse is contacted on cadence, replies on WhatsApp, and their freshness score resets — then a shift ranking reorders because of it | Shallu |
| G5 | 20 | A candidate is shortlisted, the client responds by link, an offer is signed, joining is confirmed and the placement's trial events schedule themselves | Suman + Abednego |
| G6 | 22 | A shift is checked into on the app, appears on a timesheet, is approved by a client link, and raises a shift invoice traceable to the approval in two clicks | Shallu + finance |
| G7 | 26 | A permanent invoice fires from a day-90 record, and the funnel report covers a period that has already passed | Suman |

**Acceptance is tied to the gate, not to milestone completion.** The scenario catalogue is authored
jointly and finalised gate by gate. Feedback at a gate that concerns capability already inside the
agreed scope **completes** the specification; feedback that introduces new capability is a change.

## 3. The three assumptions the gates exist to settle

| Assumption | Settled at | If it is wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Recruiters accept a ranked shortlist, and their overrides imply a weighting | G2b, week 8 | `03-07` weight model is redesigned while it is still cheap |
| A hospital contact will click a link to confirm retention at day 90 | G2, week 8 | The entire permanent billing chain needs another trigger; found in month 2 rather than month 6 |
| Candidates install the app | G4, week 16 | Shift attendance has **no other route** — the contractual invoice chain has no fallback |

## 4. Category sequencing

**Nurses first, then doctors, then allied health.** Each category is taken end to end — one client, one
requisition, one candidate sourced, ranked, shortlisted, placed, tracked to day 90 and invoiced —
before any part of it is thickened. Module coherence is a secondary aim and gives way to getting a
path working.

Category is never a code branch. Adding doctors is: reference data (`02-03`), an attribute set
(`03-04`), a registry adapter (`02-13`), a weight set (`03-07`), a trial question set (`03-09`) and,
uniquely for doctors, privileging (`03-09`, `M8-I10`). All configuration except privileging.

```
02-01 identity ─▶ 02-06 shell ─▶ 03-01 clients ─▶ 03-03 requisitions ─▶ 03-07 ranking
                                       │                                     │
                       03-04 candidates ┘                    02-14 links ────┤
                                       │                                     ▼
                       03-05 app ──────┴──────────────────────────▶ 03-08 selection
                                                                              │
                                                              03-09 placement ─┤
                                                                              ▼
                                              03-10 timesheets ──▶ 03-11 billing ──▶ 03-12 reporting
                                                                              │
                                                             03-17 payroll ◀───┴─ 03-15, 03-16, 03-19
```

**The back half is compressed and this is the main schedule risk.** Five of the ten build steps begin
in months five and six, each needing the one before it, so they cannot be parallelised by adding
people. Two consequences, stated now:

1. Any slip in months one to four lands **entirely on the Phase 6–7 work**, because it is the only
   thing left to cut.
2. **03-17 payroll sits last behind three other components.** If the contractual line matters
   commercially, it is the item most exposed to everything upstream.

If the period cannot hold both three category modules and the full R2 remainder, the client's stated
priority is **three category modules on a thinner R2**, because the category dimension is what becomes
expensive to retrofit — it reaches into the adapters, the weight model and the trial question sets.
