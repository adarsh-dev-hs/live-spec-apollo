## 1. Purpose

Part 1 proved that one line of business can run from a vacancy to a paid invoice. Part 2 opens **every
other path the platform has to carry**, and closes the second money path.

Shift staffing runs end to end — shifts offered and accepted on the app, checked into, turned into a
timesheet, approved by the client and invoiced. Vacancies stop being typed and start arriving by email
and WhatsApp, filling themselves in, with missing details chased on the channel they came from.
Candidates are contacted on a schedule and the platform learns who is genuinely available. Doctors and
allied health go live with their own councils, specialty fields, ranking weights and trial questions.
Home healthcare runs beside hospital. Clients log in and see their own vacancies. Associates are
onboarded with their statutory forms, ready for a payroll engine that is connected in Part 3.

**None of these is at full width.** Width is Part 3.

### Explicitly out of scope

- **Every intake channel except email and WhatsApp text.** Spreadsheets in client formats, voice notes
  and HRMS feeds are Part 3.
- **Voice campaigns and automated calling.** Part 3.
- **Every report.** Part 3.
- **The migration itself.** Only profiling happens here.
- **Payroll.** Associates are onboarded and made ready; no pay run exists.
- **Raising a vacancy from the portal**, portal shortlists, portal timesheet approval and portal
  invoices. Part 3.
- Collections, workforce changes, prioritisation, leave and overtime.

### Explicitly not built

- A second attendance route. **The app is the only way a shift check-in reaches the platform**, and it
  stays that way. Externally uploaded attendance in Part 3 is a pairing exercise, not a second route.
- Automatic merging of anything. Suspected duplicates are surfaced for a human in both Part 2 and Part 3.
