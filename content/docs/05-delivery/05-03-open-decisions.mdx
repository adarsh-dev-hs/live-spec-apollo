## 2. High-impact non-blockers

Cheap to decide now, expensive to retrofit.

| ID | Question | Impact | Working assumption |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`OI-HS-01`** | Does a site-resident recruiter see only their own site, or the whole base? | **Row-level scoping is a different build from screen-level permissions and touches every list in the platform.** | **`AS-02`** — build the scope mechanism from day one, applied in every repository, defaulting `recruiter` to `all`. Switching is then a config change, not 28 query rewrites. |
| **`OI-A-12`** | Are link recipients held per client, location, requisition or placement? | **Without a recipient model the day-90 billing trigger is not defensible.** | **`AS-05`** — `client_contact` with a `purposes` array, per client + optional location. `02-10` creates `link_recipient` rows from these; `03-11` denormalises the respondent onto the retention record so the evidence survives whatever the model becomes. |
| **`OI-A-11`** | The ranking weight model across credentials, specialty, availability and outcomes | **The largest unspecified design in Release 1.** 3 categories × 2 bases multiplies the sets. | **`AS-32`** — a provisional weight set, fully configurable, plus a **simulator** so Apollo tunes against real requisitions during UAT. **Run a weighting workshop in phase 4.** |
| **`OI-HS-10`** | Does an expired credential / pending verification / stale availability **exclude** or **reduce score**? | *"The difference between a filter and a weight changes what a recruiter sees and what can be defended to a client."* | **`AS-27`** — configured per condition. Default: expired or red registration **excludes**; pending verification and stale availability **reduce**. |
| **`OI-HS-06`** | IDfy or AuthBridge, and per-check pricing | Coverage and response schema differ; **late selection means adapter rework** | Build the port against the union of both. Selection becomes a config row. |
| **`OI-HS-08`** | Native or cross-platform candidate app | **Store review cycles are calendar time; the app is on the revenue path** | **`AS-28`** — React Native. **Start Apple enrolment month 2 — it needs a D-U-N-S in Apollo's name.** |
| **`OI-HS-02`** | DPDP erasure against the indefinite Retained Pool | A subject erasure request and indefinite retention cannot both be honoured | **`AS-08`** — erasure **anonymises in place**; statutory records keep the transaction facts, not the identity (`01-10 §6`). **Needs Apollo legal sign-off.** |
| **`OI-HS-14`** | Finance retention for billing records vs DPDP candidate retention | A billing record must survive years; a candidate may ask for erasure sooner | Same, `AS-08`. The day-90 evidence rests on the **client-side** respondent, who is not the data subject — which is what makes it survivable. |
| **`OI-HS-03`** | Mapping Apollo's 34 deliverables to the 24 capability blocks in the signed SOW | *"The two documents describe different releases and will drift within weeks."* | **Commercial, not technical.** Raise before this plan is baselined. |
| **`OI-HS-04`** | Is "client-hosted AWS Mumbai" an Apollo-owned account? | Changes who holds credentials, who is on call, how deploys are approved | **`AS-13`** — Apollo-owned; Hiresense via cross-account role; **no Hiresense credential at runtime after handover.** Blocks phase 0. |

## 4. Gaps we found that neither document raises

Not in the plan's register. Raised here because they are real.

| Gap | Impact | Where | Proposed answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Consent for migrated CPAL candidates** | CPAL arrives with **no consent evidence**. `03-06` will refuse to run verification checks on the migrated base — **which is most of the pool at go-live.** | `03-15 §11`, `01-10 §9` | **`AS-44`** — re-consent campaign via `03-08`, or a documented legitimate-use position from Apollo legal, **before cutover** |
| **GST treatment on invoices** | Nothing specifies place of supply, rates or reverse charge. **An invoice with wrong GST is a compliance problem, not a bug.** | `03-13 §11` | **`AS-40`** — fields exist, rates from configuration. **Apollo finance must specify before go-live.** |
| **Reporting has no history before go-live** | Reports can only cover the period since events began. **No backfill exists.** | `03-14 §11` | State the coverage window on every report; emit synthetic migration events where dates are usable (**`AS-42`**) |
| **Timesheet export for manual payroll** | Related to `OI-A-15` but broader — payroll runs manually for all of R1 with no defined artefact | `03-12 §11` | **`AS-37`** — build it. Small, obviously needed. |
| **Invoice numbering series** | Indian statutory practice may require per-series numbering | `03-13 §11` | **`AS-41`** — `INV-YYYY-NNNNNN`, gapless. Confirm with finance. |
| **Delegation while an approver is on leave** | A pending approval with no available approver stalls a pay run | `02-11 §11` | Build simple date-range delegation, audited |
| **Report access scope** | Should a recruiter see other recruiters' performance? A management decision, not a technical one. | `03-14 §11` | **`AS-43`** — leadership/admin all; lead sees their team; recruiter sees own. **Sensitive default either way — confirm.** |

## 6. Review cadence

| When | Do |
|---|---|
| **Weekly** | Chase the seven estimate blockers (`05-02 §11`). Report movement, or the absence of it. |
| **Each phase gate** | Re-check every open item touching that phase; confirm assumptions still hold |
| **When an item closes** | Update this register, update the affected specs, **assess the blast radius via the `AS-` index above**, raise a scope change if the answer differs from the assumption |
| **Before baselining** | `OI-HS-03` — the deliverables-to-SOW mapping. *"The two documents describe different projects."* |

**An open item that is still open at its "needed by" date is a schedule risk, not an admin task.**
Report it as one.
